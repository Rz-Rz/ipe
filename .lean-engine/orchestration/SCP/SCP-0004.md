# SCP-0004: Line endings through the editor

## Intent

SPEC-PRJ-006 requires a unit saved by the product to reopen identical to what was saved, and SPEC-PRJ-001 requires that adopting a folder rewrites no file. ENG-0003's editor keeps every byte of a unit: a unit whose line breaks are all CRLF is split and joined on CRLF, any other unit on LF with stray carriage returns kept as characters; a line break the author types follows the unit's separator. The requirements do not say what separator a typed line break takes in a unit that mixes CRLF and LF, nor whether the product may ever normalise. This change states the policy.

## Scope and non-goals

Touches the coverage of SPEC-PRJ-006 and the limit case of SPEC-PRJ-001 in `dossier-recette/11_PROJECT_STRUCTURE.md`. Does not change the storage, which writes bytes as given.

## Proposed modification

Option A (recommended, as delivered): coverage of SPEC-PRJ-006, limit: "a unit keeps its bytes through the editor whatever its line endings; a line break the author types is CRLF in a unit whose line breaks are all CRLF and LF otherwise; a unit that mixes the two is announced when shown." Limit case of SPEC-PRJ-001: "no file rewritten, not even once shown in the editor."

Option B: the product normalises every unit to LF at the author's first save, and SPEC-PRJ-006 says so as an exclusion of the byte promise.

## Example

An author opens a serial written on Windows: every chapter keeps its CRLF line breaks, the new lines they type end with CRLF too, and the files compare equal in their other tool.

## Recommendation

Option A: byte identity is the promise of Q06, and it costs nothing now that the editor keeps it.
