# SCP-0003: What the search panel lists when the occurrences exceed what fits

## Intent

SPEC-PRJ-005 requires the product to find a text string across all manuscript units and all objects and to show each hit with its unit and passage. ENG-0003 delivered a kernel that returns every occurrence and an interface that lists the first 200 with the total ("showing 200 of N hits; narrow the search to see the rest"). A list of every occurrence of a common word in a long serial runs to thousands of entries; the requirement must say whether every hit is listed or the first N with the total.

## Scope and non-goals

Touches the requirement sentence and the coverage of SPEC-PRJ-005 in `dossier-recette/11_PROJECT_STRUCTURE.md`. Does not change the kernel's search, which returns every occurrence.

## Proposed modification

Current requirement: "The product must find a text string across all manuscript units and all objects of the project and must show each hit with its unit and passage."

Option A: "... and must list every hit with its unit and passage." The panel lists every occurrence (a virtualised list for long results).

Option B (recommended, as delivered): "... and must show the hits with their unit and passage, the first 200 listed and the total count stated when the list is cut." Coverage, limit: "a search with more than 200 occurrences lists the first 200 and states the total; narrowing the search reaches the rest."

| Test | Artefact | Nature | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| TST-PRJ-005b | `e2e/prj.spec.ts` > SPEC-PRJ-005 Project search > TST-PRJ-005b | e2e | The panel lists every hit when they fit, and the first 200 with the total when they do not. |

## Example

An author searches "the" in a forty-chapter serial: the panel lists 200 hits and says "showing 200 of 6 412 hits"; searching "the ford" lists the 9 hits.

## Recommendation

Option B: nothing is left out in silence, the total is always true, and the panel stays usable; option A costs a virtualised list for a case where the author will narrow the search anyway.
